# Spontaneous CP violation: turning Delta_CP = 3 delta_2 − 2 delta_3 into a calculable quantity

**Status:** YES, with stated caveats. Spontaneous CP breaking in a minimal
flavon sector makes Delta_CP **calculable and generically O(1)**: the minimal
Z_2 model gives Delta_CP = pi/2 exactly at its symmetric point, and over a
scan of the real coefficients (SCPV region) **median |Delta_CP| = 1.572 rad,
16–84% = [1.070, 2.067]**, i.e. sin(Delta_CP/2) median **0.708** [0.510,
0.859]. It is a *generic O(1) phase peaked at pi/2*, not a sharp single
number, except at the symmetric point. Two structural no-gos are proven along
the way, and the two classic diseases (CP domain walls, strong CP) are
quantified: the flavon walls **must be inflated away** (a window exists), and
theta_bar is protected Nelson–Barr-style by the *same* messenger sequestering
the model already needs — with one real tension (gravity-induced dim-6)
forcing a choice between two branches.

**Module:** `spontaneous_cp.py` (runs under the repo venv; every number below
is pasted from its output). Outputs: `spontaneous_cp_scan.csv` (40 000 draws),
`spontaneous_cp_summary.csv`, `delta_cp_distribution.png`,
`walls_and_etaB.png`.

## 1. Setup: CP exact, broken by the flavon VEV

CP is imposed on the Lagrangian: **all couplings real**. The harmonic
couplings are generated by a complex flavon S = v_S e^{i theta_S}:

    kappa_2 = c_2 (S/M_f)^{n_2},   kappa_3 = c_3 (S/M_f)^{n_3},   c_2, c_3 real,

so delta_2 = n_2 theta_S, delta_3 = n_3 theta_S (mod pi from sign(c_i)), and

    Delta_CP = (3 n_2 − 2 n_3) theta_S  ≡  m · theta_S .

The S_3 hardening result (`S3_HARDENING_REPORT.md`) guarantees the phases pass
through the doublet contraction untouched (real CG coefficients, slopes
+3/−2), so fixing arg kappa_2, arg kappa_3 fixes Delta_CP exactly. The
magnitudes |kappa_2| = 1.43e−4, |kappa_3| are absorbed into c_i, v_S/M_f
(Sec. 5); **only the phase is at issue here.**

### 1.1 Two structural no-go theorems (Exact/structural, verified in code)

**(N1) A single U(1)_Q-charged flavon cannot generate Delta_CP.**
If S restores the rephasing symmetry (n_2 q_S = −2 q_P, n_3 q_S = −3 q_P),
then n_2/n_3 = 2/3 and m = 3n_2 − 2n_3 = 0 **identically**; Delta_CP = 0, and
theta_S is the U(1)_Q Goldstone (phase potential exactly flat). The flavon
charge must be a *flavor* symmetry independent of U(1)_Q.

**(N2) A single Z_N-invariant harmonic gives only geometric, CP-conserving
phases.** V = b v^4 cos(N theta) has vacua at theta = pi j/N; consistency of
the Z_N in the P sector requires m ≡ 0 mod N, so Delta_CP = pi j (m/N) ∈
{0, pi} mod 2pi. Verified numerically for Z_2, Z_3, Z_4, Z_6 and both signs
of b: **Delta_CP ∈ {0.0000, 3.1416} in every case.** Geometric phases cancel
inside the invariant 3delta_2 − 2delta_3.

Note **Delta_CP = pi is also CP-conserving** despite sin(Delta_CP/2) = 1:
there J_CP = Im(kappa_2^3 kappa_3*^2) = sin(Delta_CP) = 0, no basis with both
couplings real exists, but a generalized CP (CP ∘ rephasing) survives and is
broken only by the *choice of psi minimum*. The two psi minima give ±n_pair,
they are exactly degenerate (the A_3 bias ∝ cos(Delta_CP/2) vanishes, Sec. 3),
and the asymmetry **averages to zero over unbiased psi domains** — the wall
analysis and the CP counting close consistently. So the useful range is
Delta_CP ∉ {0, pi}, and *frustration* of the flavon phase is mandatory.

## 2. Minimal working model and its prediction

**Model (Model assumption):** one flavon S, flavor Z_2 (S → −S), CP exact.
The most general renormalizable potential with real couplings:

    V(S) = −m² |S|² + λ|S|⁴ + [ (m'²/2) S² + (λ'/4) S⁴ + (λ''/2)|S|² S² + h.c. ].

With x = cos 2theta_S, radially minimized (exact, used in the code):

    V_min(x) = −mu²(x)² / (4 Λ(x)),   mu² = m² − m'² x,
    Λ = λ + λ'(2x² − 1)/2 + λ'' x .

Phase-only form: V_theta = a cos 2theta + b cos 4theta, a = m'²v² + λ''v⁴,
b = λ'v⁴/2. The frustrated minimum

    cos 2theta_S* = −a/(4b)    (exists iff b > 0 and |a| < 4b)

has sin 2theta_S* ≠ 0: **spontaneous CPV with a calculable phase** (verified:
exact 2-d minimization agrees with −a/4b to 5.9e−8 at the benchmark point).

**Embeddings** (Z_2 consistency forces n_2 even, hence m even):
(n_2, n_3) = (2,2) → m = 2 (minimal, headline); (2,1) → m = 4;
(2,3) → m = 0 (the commensurate no-go N1). m is a **discrete model choice**;
theta_S* is calculable given the real couplings.

**Computed predictions (Derived):**

| quantity | value |
|---|---|
| symmetric point (m'² = λ'' = 0, λ' > 0): theta* | pi/4 **exactly** → **Delta_CP = pi/2 exactly** (m = 2), sin(Delta_CP/2) = 0.7071 |
| benchmark (m'² = 0.3, λ' = 0.5, λ'' = −0.2), m = 2 | Delta_CP = 1.8400 rad, sin(Delta_CP/2) = 0.7956 |
| same point, m = 4 | Delta_CP = 2.6032 rad, sin = 0.9640 |

Caveat (honest): a = 0 is **not radiatively stable** (λ'' ~ λλ'/16pi² is
regenerated), so pi/2 is the *central value of a distribution*, not a
protected prediction.

## 3. Scan: is Delta_CP robust or model-dependent?

40 000 draws, couplings m'², λ', λ'' = (random sign) × 10^U, U uniform in
[−2, 0], λ = m² = 1, boundedness enforced (50.7% of raw draws bounded).
Global minimum found by exact radial reduction + 4001-point grid + parabolic
refinement (`spontaneous_cp_scan.csv`).

**SCPV fraction: 21.9%** of coefficient space (43.2% given the frustrating
sign λ' > 0; **0%** for λ' < 0 — the potential is then convex in cos2theta and
the vacuum locks to theta ∈ {0, pi/2}: CP-conserving). So spontaneous CPV is
a *sign condition plus |a| < 4b*, not automatic — stated plainly.

**Within the SCPV region (m = 2, N = 8773):**

    |Delta_CP|:      median 1.572 rad,  16–84% [1.070, 2.067],  5–95% [0.645, 2.493]
    sin(|dCP|/2):    median 0.708,      16–84% [0.510, 0.859]
    P(sin > 0.3) = 95.7%;   P(within 0.2 rad of 0 or pi) = 0.8%

The density of Delta_CP is peaked at pi/2 and vanishes toward the
CP-conserving endpoints (because cos Delta_CP = −a/4b is smoothly distributed:
endpoint values require tuned |a| = 4b). For m = 4: median 2.554 rad,
sin median 0.957, but 23.3% of draws land within 0.2 rad of pi — the minimal
m = 2 embedding is also the cosmologically safest (Sec. 4a).

**eta_B consequence** (calibrated forward model of `predict_eta_B.py`,
eta_3 = 1): median eta_B = **1.21e−9**, 16–84% [8.71e−10, 1.47e−9], vs
observed 6.10e−10 — the spontaneous phase keeps the natural prediction within
a factor ~2, and *removes sin(Delta_CP/2) as a free input* (it can no longer
be dialed below ~0.3 except in 4% of the SCPV region).

**Verdict on robustness (honest):** Delta_CP is **calculable but not a sharp
universal number**. What is robust: (i) generically O(1), peaked at pi/2;
(ii) strongly suppressed near 0 and pi; (iii) exactly pi/2 at the symmetric
point. What is model-dependent: the discrete multiplier m (flavor charges),
the SCPV sign condition (λ' > 0), and the coefficient measure. The honest
headline: *"spontaneous CP gives a calculable O(1) phase, typically far from
0 or pi, with central value pi/2."*

## 4. The two classic diseases

### 4a. Domain walls

**psi-walls (the existing Z_2 network, `DARK_SECTOR_AND_WALL_REPORT.md`):**
the shipped analysis used bias delta_V = 2A_3, implicitly |cos(Delta_CP/2)|
= 1. The exact bias between the two A_2 minima (psi = 0, pi; basis delta_2 =
0, delta_3 = −Delta_CP/2) is

    delta_V = 2 A_3 |cos(Delta_CP/2)|   =>   margin(Delta_CP) = 6.28 · sqrt(|cos(Delta_CP/2)|).

So **the wall bias and the baryon asymmetry are complementary**
(cos vs sin of the same angle): maximal CP (Delta_CP = pi) *kills the wall
bias* — a new, quantitative reason the "assume maximal CP" input was never
safe. Computed exclusion: **unsafe (margin < 1) iff |Delta_CP| > 3.0909 rad**
(within 0.051 of pi). At the median spontaneous prediction (1.572 rad) the
margin is **5.28×**; only **0.01%** of SCPV draws are unsafe. The spontaneous
mechanism lands the model in the safe-and-baryogenic band automatically
(`walls_and_etaB.png`, right panel of `delta_cp_distribution.png`).

**Flavon CP walls (new):** SCPV creates exactly degenerate vacua ±theta_S*
(CP partners; the Z_2 image theta+pi is physically identical for even
embeddings). Tension sigma_S = 0.618 v_S³ at the benchmark point (computed by
integrating over the lower barrier). **No bias is possible**: CP exact means
every operator, including Planck-suppressed ones, has real coefficients and
is even in theta_S — the degeneracy is exact to all orders. If Kibble-formed
they dominate (branch A: at T = 4.7e13 GeV) and never collapse:
**inflation is the only resolution.** Requirement: T_RH < T_S ~ v_S while
T_RH ≥ T_n = 3.17e12 GeV (the bath must reach the nucleation temperature):

| branch | v_S | T_RH window | factor |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| A (c_2 = 1, M_f = M_cut = 4e17) | 4.79e15 GeV | [3.2e12, 4.8e15] GeV | **1510** (comfortable) |
| B (theta_bar-safe, Sec. 4b) | 2.44e13 GeV | [3.2e12, 2.4e13] GeV | **7.7** (tight but open) |

This also fixes homogeneity: an inflated flavon phase is uniform, so the
*sign* of Delta_CP (chosen spontaneously between ±theta_S*) is uniform across
the observable universe — |eta_B| is predicted, its sign is environmental
(standard for SCPV). The psi-wall history of the shipped analysis is
unchanged (those walls form at T_n, after reheating, and are biased as above).

### 4b. Strong CP / Nelson–Barr

- **theta_bar(tree) = 0 exactly** (Exact/structural): CP exact forces
  theta_QCD = 0 and real quark Yukawas; the flavon has no tree-level coupling
  to colored fields. This is a Nelson–Barr-type arrangement: CP broken only
  in a sector sequestered from quarks.
- **Radiative feed-through** via the messenger bridge (the S3_HARDENING
  working point y_b y_d = 2.55e−3, m_mess = 1.98e16 GeV):
  theta_bar ~ (y_b y_d/16pi²)² (rho/m_mess)² sin Delta_CP = **6.9e−17** ≪
  1e−10. **The same messenger sequestering already mandated by thermal
  survival (channel-D ceiling) protects theta_bar for free.** (Derived
  benchmark; O(1) loop factors not computed.)
- **The one real tension — gravity-induced dim-6** (Open precision problem):
  if quantum gravity generates (S²/M_Pl²) q̄ H q with an O(1) real
  coefficient, theta_bar ~ (v_S/M_Pl)² sin Delta_CP. Branch A (v_S = 4.8e15):
  **3.9e−6 — FAILS** the 1e−10 bound. Two viable resolutions, both with a
  cost:
  - **Branch A + gauged Z_N:** gauge the flavor symmetry with N ≥ 4 and
    quarks neutral, so the smallest invariant power gives (v_S/M_Pl)^n with
    n ≥ 4 (computed: n ≥ 4 suffices). Cost: a gauged Z_4 forbids the
    renormalizable S² frustration terms, so the frustrated potential then
    needs a **second flavon** (e.g. two Z_4-charged flavons with
    renormalizable cross-couplings) — one step less minimal, same mechanism.
  - **Branch B — lower v_S:** v_S = sqrt(1e−10) M_Pl = 2.44e13 GeV saturates
    the bound (theta_bar ~ 1.0e−10, marginal by construction). |kappa_2| =
    1.43e−4 then requires the flavon mediator at M_f = v_S/sqrt(kappa_2) =
    **2.0e15 GeV < M_cut** — consistent (M_f is a new scale distinct from the
    m_1-protection cutoff), but the inflation window shrinks to 7.7× (4a) and
    theta_bar sits at the observable edge (a *prediction*: nEDM within ~1
    order).

Neither disease is swept under the rug: the walls have a genuine requirement
(T_RH < v_S) that is comfortable in branch A and tight in branch B, and the
strong-CP problem forces branch A to gauge the flavor symmetry (second
flavon) or branch B to live at the edge of the nEDM bound.
