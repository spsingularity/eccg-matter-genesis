## 1. The requirement (referee M3, quantified)

Quantum gravity violates global symmetries. If the S_3 that forbids bare
P = Φ_V Φ_D and the cross-Kähler X_2†X_3 P is **global**, a dim-5 Planck operator
regenerates

    m_1/H ~ (ρ/M_Pl)^{(d-4)/2} (m_2/H) :  d=5 → 23.9 (FATAL),  d=6 → 0.049 (OK),

with ρ = 1.02e13 GeV, m_2/H = 1.17e4 (reproduced in code). So the protection must
be a **discrete gauge symmetry**. [Derived]

## 2. Anomaly conditions used (statement)

For a non-Abelian discrete group the anomaly of the fermion measure under g ∈ G is
the **determinant phase** det D_f(g) of each fermion's rep, weighted by the index
of its continuous-gauge rep (AKKO+). With ℓ(R) = 2T(R) (ℓ(fund) = 1) and
N = ord(g), the 't Hooft-vertex conditions are

    Z_N–G_a–G_a :   A_a(g) = Σ_f q_f^det(g) ℓ(R_a,f) · dim(other reps) ≡ 0  (mod N)
    Z_N–grav–grav:  A_gr(g) = Σ_f q_f^det(g) · dim(R_f)                ≡ 0  (mod N)

(the gravitational condition via the K3 instanton, Dirac index 2 per Weyl fermion,
is 2Σq ≡ 0 mod N — vacuous for N = 2, equivalent to strict for N = 3;
Ω^Spin_5(BZ_2) = 0 confirms the Z_2–grav condition is empty). Z_N–U(1)² is imposed
with quantized charges; U(1)–Z_N² is **not well defined** without a UV charge lift
(Banks–Dine; AKKO+ discard it). It is enough to check the cyclic subgroups
Z_3 = ⟨r⟩ and Z_2 = ⟨s⟩ of S_3. [Exact/structural — cited framework]

## 3. Structural theorem (verified in code, Part 1)

det: S_3 → U(1) factors through the abelianization S_3/[S_3,S_3] = Z_2. Explicitly
(computed): det = +1 on {e, r, r²} in **every** irrep (1, 1′, 2); det(s) = −1 for
1′ and 2. Hence

    **every Z_3–G_a–G_a and Z_3–grav discrete anomaly of full S_3 multiplets
    vanishes identically, for any field content**,

and only the Z_2(s) conditions — and the Z_3 conditions of a Z_3⋊Z_2 realization
with incomplete multiplets — are nontrivial. Immediate corollary: **no instanton
of any gauge group can violate the Z_3 ⊂ S_3** (its 't Hooft vertices carry zero
Z_3 charge), to all orders. [Exact/structural]

## 4. Correction to the collective_breaking selection rules (Part 2–3)

The real-character singlet counting in `collective_breaking.py` misidentified two
operators. Building Sym^k(2) explicitly and projecting (code, exact):

    deg-2 singlet = x² + y² = |P|²        (NOT the 2nd harmonic Re P²)
    deg-3 singlet = x³ − 3xy² = Re(P³)    (the 3rd harmonic, NOT P²P†)

With the Z_3 grading r: P → ωP (s: P → P†), the holomorphic charges are:

| operator | Z_3 | Z_31 | S_3 | Z_31 | both |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|---|
| P (m_1) | 1 | 1 | forbidden | forbidden | forbidden |
| P² (m_2) | 2 | 2 | **forbidden** | forbidden | forbidden |
| P³ (m_3) | 0 | 3 | allowed | forbidden | forbidden |
| P²P† ("residual m_1") | 1 | 1 | **forbidden** | forbidden | forbidden |
| X_2 P² | 0 | 0 | allowed (X_2 must be a **doublet**) | allowed | allowed |

| X_2†X_3 P (Kähler cross) | 0 | 0 | **allowed** | allowed | allowed |
| X_2² P (renormalizable W) | 0 | 28 | **allowed** | forbidden | forbidden |

Consequences (all verified by the exhaustive scan, `s3_first_harmonic_scan.csv`):

1. **Good:** gauged S_3 forbids the pure-P first harmonic to **all orders** (the
   invariant ring of the doublet is generated by |P|² and Re P³) — stronger than
   the report claimed; the advertised "residual P²P†" does not exist.
2. **Bad:** it also forbids the *required* bare second harmonic, so X_2 is forced
   into the S_3 doublet — and then X_2†X_3 P is S_3-invariant again. **The
   non-Abelian defeat of the Abelian Kähler no-go does not survive the corrected
   analysis**; protection of m_1 is quantitative (small F_2, F_3), not exact.
3. **Fatal for S_3-alone:** the renormalizable superpotential term X_2²P is
   S_3-invariant. It gives m_1/H = 64·√λ, i.e. needs λ < 2.2e-5 — an unprotected
   tuning. **Gauged Z_31 forbids X_2²P exactly** (charge −3), and also the
   1-insertion Kähler X_2†P (charge 3). Z_31 is load-bearing; S_3 alone is not
   viable. [Exact/structural for the charges; Derived for the numbers]

Z_31; ⟨Z_31, Z_3, s⟩ = **D_93 = Z_93 ⋊ Z_2** (Z_93 = Z_31 × Z_3, coprime), not

2. **If S_3 is kept** (e.g. for the 14→4 completion economy — which must anyway be
   re-audited against Section 4): gauge **D_93 ⊂ O(2)_P** — a single U(1)_P
   (q(P) = 1... via CRT lift) broken by a charge-93 scalar, with the reflection as
   **gauged generalized CP** (natural here: ECCG already breaks CP spontaneously
   via the flavon sector, as gauged CP requires). Costs: the 4-pair Z_3 spectator
   set (R3) or the 7-superfield doubling (R2), plus the open Pin⁺ ν mod 16
   bookkeeping. All computed conditions then vanish.

## 8. Honest verdict

- **Is S_3 gaugeable?** Yes — anomaly-freedom holds identically for its Z_3 part
  (det theorem) and by explicit computation for the rest; cost is zero (R1),
  7 chiral superfields (R2), or 8 Weyl spectators + gauged-CP bookkeeping (R3).
- **Instanton/gravity safety?** Yes — no gauge, fractional, or gravitational
  instanton carries S_3 (or Z_31) charge; leading gravity-allowed m_1 operator is
  effective dim-6 (collective, 2 spurions); residual m_1/H = 5.2e-4 (model)
  / 6.7e-10 (Planck-mediated) / 0.049 (referee's most conservative bracket) — all
  < 0.3.
- **But** the corrected selection rules show S_3 is **not** the hero: it cannot
  stand alone (renormalizable X_2²P ⇒ m_1/H ~ 64√λ), and once X_2 is a doublet the
  cross-Kähler operator returns. Gauged **Z_31 is necessary and sufficient**;
  S_3, if kept, is a consistent refinement inside gauged **D_93 = Z_93 ⋊ Z_2^gCP**
  that removes all holomorphic direct-P operators below 9 insertions.
- **Action for the papers:** restate M3's resolution as "gauged Z_31 (+ optional
  D_93)", correct the collective_breaking operator table (P², P²P† rows), and
  re-audit the 14→4 parameter-reduction claim, which relied on the uncorrected
